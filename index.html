PERSPECTIVE

財富支持未來的計劃。健康資本讓你參與其中。 Financial capital helps fund the future you imagine. Health Capital helps you take part in it.

無論你追求當下的運動表現，還是數十年後的行動自主，起點都不是盲目加量，而是找出限制因子。 Whether you are pursuing performance today or independence decades from now, the starting point is not simply doing more—it is finding the limiting factor.

當訓練只依賴直覺、通用課表或短期高強度消耗，真正限制進步的因素便容易被忽略。V2 Wellness Group 將體能視作長遠的「健康資本」，連結你今日想實現的目標，與未來數十年仍想保留的生活選擇權。瓶頸不一定在意志力，也可能來自身體尚未被察覺的限制；我們會從心肺與血管、肌力與下肢、平衡與穩定、身體組成四個領域逐一檢視。先量度，再介入。 When training relies only on intuition, generic templates, or short-term intensity, the factors truly limiting progress are easily missed. At V2 Wellness Group, we treat physical capacity as long-term Health Capital—connecting what you want to achieve today with the capabilities you want to preserve for decades ahead. The bottleneck is not always willpower; it may lie in unrecognized physical constraints across your cardiorespiratory capacity, lower-body strength, balance, and body composition. We measure first, then build capacity with precision.

THE V2 METHOD

由客觀基線，到可驗證的實測變化。 From objective baseline to verified delta.

01

建立生理基線 Baseline

於可重現的標準條件下，系統化採集代謝能力、身體結構、力量輸出與動作機能基線。 Comprehensive physiological and functional screening across metabolic capacity, body composition, strength, and movement mechanics—conducted under repeatable conditions.

02

鎖定限制因子 Bottleneck

對照已發表之常模研究，建構個人「健康資本指數（HCI）」，鎖定當下首要限制因子。 Benchmarked against published normative research to construct your Health Capital Index (HCI), isolating the primary bottleneck that governs your next phase.

03

介入與系統修復 Intervention

數據本身並非方案。由專屬教練結合客觀指標與專業判斷制定階段性訓練，並僅在多項生理指標達標時，審慎配合被動修復模組。 Data alone is not a program. Your dedicated coach synthesizes objective findings into a phased training plan, integrating targeted recovery modalities strictly when physiological indicators warrant them.

04

同條件複測驗證 Reassessment

每 16 堂為一個完整週期，透過同條件複測核對實測數值與真實變化（Δ），並標註可比性等級，絕不以百分比粉飾結果。 Every 16-session cycle concludes with a matched-condition re-assessment, reporting raw measured values and actual change (Δ) with explicit comparability ratings—never inflated percentages.

THE INDEX

四個領域，一個指數。 Four domains. One index.

四個核心領域中的指定評分錨點會匯聚成你的「健康資本指數（HCI）」——以 0 至 100 分綜合呈現，四個領域權重均等，並對照已發表之常模研究定位。這四個領域構成核心機能基線；表現與代謝上限方向按需要疊加的進階代謝與力量測試，僅作補充評估及教練判斷，不納入指數計分。 Designated scoring anchors across four core domains form your Health Capital Index (HCI)—a composite score from 0 to 100, equally weighted across the domains and positioned against published normative research. These four domains establish the core capacity baseline. Advanced metabolic and strength testing may be added for the performance pathway to support supplementary assessment and coaching judgment; these results are not included in the index score.

01

心肺與血管 Cardiorespiratory & Vascular

六分鐘步行測試與一分鐘心率恢復——真實負荷下的循環耐力。 Six-minute walk and one-minute heart-rate recovery—circulatory capacity under real demand.

02

肌力與下肢 Strength & Lower Body

三十秒坐站測試與握力——下肢功能及整體力量的標準化參考指標。 Thirty-second sit-to-stand and grip strength—standardized markers of lower-body function and overall strength.

03

平衡與穩定 Balance & Stability

單腳站立測試——神經肌肉控制與姿勢穩定度。 Single-leg stance—neuromuscular control and postural stability.

04

身體組成 Body Composition

腰圍、骨骼肌量與體脂率——身體結構的長期走勢。 Waist circumference, skeletal muscle mass, and body-fat percentage—the structural trajectory.

60

常模下限——同齡群第 25 百分位，正常範圍的起點；低於此線即落後同齡水平。 The lower normative bound—the 25th percentile of your age band; below it, you trail the normal range.

85

機能儲備——同齡群第 75 百分位；以上代表超越日常負荷的安全邊際，常模並非終點。 Functional reserve—the 75th percentile of your age band; above it lies capacity beyond daily demand. The norm is not the finish line.

LOWEST

分數旁永遠並列最低分領域——限制因子所在，正是介入的起點。 The lowest domain is always named beside your score—where the limiting factor sits is where the work begins.

TWICE

指數只發佈兩次：初步基線評估，以及第 16 堂的同條件複測。同一條件下測得的變化，才是真正的進步。 The index is published twice only: at your initial assessment, and at your matched-condition re-assessment on session sixteen. Change under identical conditions is the only change that counts.

GOVERNANCE

透明標準，真人把關。 Transparent standards. Human accountability.

所有常模錨點均附公開文獻來源；實測數據與推算數值嚴格分級標示。 Every normative benchmark cites its published source. Measured values are strictly separated from estimates.

FAQ

預約前常見問題。 Before your first visit.

01 首次到訪會進行什麼？ What happens on the first visit? +

首次到訪為初步基線評估。我們會在可重現的標準條件下，採集你的心肺耐力、肌力輸出、平衡穩定與身體組成四個領域的客觀基線，並由專屬教練解讀個人「健康資本指數（HCI）」及鎖定首要限制因子，作為後續方案的依據。 Your first visit is an Initial Baseline Assessment. Under repeatable conditions, we measure objective baselines across cardiorespiratory capacity, strength, balance, and body composition. Your coach then reviews your Health Capital Index (HCI) and isolates the primary limiting factor to guide your next phase.

02 收費與方案如何計算？ How are programs and fees structured? +

我們不設劃一散堂價目表。所有方案均於完成初步基線評估後，按你的實測結果、目標及行程度身報價，並以每 16 堂為一個完整週期運作（包含期末同條件複測驗證）；初步基線評估之費用可於查詢時直接向團隊確認。 We do not publish a generic drop-in menu. Programs are quoted after your Initial Baseline Assessment, tailored to your measured baselines, goals, and schedule, and structured in 16-session cycles that conclude with a matched-condition re-assessment. Entry assessment fees are confirmed directly upon inquiry.

03 如何預約初步評估，或為家人轉介？ How do I book an assessment, or refer a family member? +
