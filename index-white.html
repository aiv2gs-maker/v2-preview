PERSPECTIVE

財富支持未來的計劃。健康資本讓你參與其中。 Financial capital helps fund the future you imagine. Health Capital helps you take part in it.

無論你追求當下的運動表現，還是數十年後的行動自主，起點都不是盲目加量，而是找出限制因子。 Whether you are pursuing performance today or independence decades from now, the starting point is not simply doing more—it is finding the limiting factor.

當訓練只依賴直覺、通用課表或短期高強度消耗，真正限制進步的因素便容易被忽略。V2 Wellness Group 將體能視作長遠的「健康資本」，連結你今日想實現的目標，與未來數十年仍想保留的生活選擇權。瓶頸不一定在意志力，也可能來自身體尚未被察覺的限制；我們會從心肺與血管、肌力與下肢、平衡與穩定、身體組成四個領域逐一檢視。先量度，再介入。 When training relies only on intuition, generic templates, or short-term intensity, the factors truly limiting progress are easily missed. At V2 Wellness Group, we treat physical capacity as long-term Health Capital—connecting what you want to achieve today with the capabilities you want to preserve for decades ahead. The bottleneck is not always willpower; it may lie in unrecognized physical constraints across your cardiorespiratory capacity, lower-body strength, balance, and body composition. We measure first, then build capacity with precision.

THE INDEX

四個領域，一個指數。 Four domains. One index.

四個核心領域中的指定評分錨點會匯聚成你的「健康資本指數（HCI）」——以 0 至 100 分綜合呈現，四個領域權重均等，並對照已發表之常模研究定位。這四個領域構成核心機能基線；表現與代謝上限方向按需要疊加的進階代謝與力量測試，僅作補充評估及教練判斷，不納入指數計分。 Designated scoring anchors across four core domains form your Health Capital Index (HCI)—a composite score from 0 to 100, equally weighted across the domains and positioned against published normative research. These four domains establish the core capacity baseline. Advanced metabolic and strength testing may be added for the performance pathway to support supplementary assessment and coaching judgment; these results are not included in the index score.

儀器原生綜合分數不作計分依據；單日狀態及中心以外監測，另作過程記錄，不納入指數。 Proprietary device summary scores are not used; single-day state and out-of-centre monitoring are recorded separately as process data and do not affect the score.

01

心肺與血管 Cardiorespiratory & Vascular

六分鐘步行測試與一分鐘心率恢復——真實負荷下的循環耐力。 Six-minute walk and one-minute heart-rate recovery—circulatory capacity under real demand.

02

肌力與下肢 Strength & Lower Body

三十秒坐站測試與握力——下肢功能及整體力量的標準化指標。 Thirty-second sit-to-stand and grip strength—standardized markers of lower-body function and overall strength.

03

平衡與穩定 Balance & Stability

單腳站立測試——神經肌肉控制與姿勢穩定度。 Single-leg stance—neuromuscular control and postural stability.

04

身體組成 Body Composition

腰圍、骨骼肌量與體脂率——體重以外的身體結構走勢。 Waist circumference, skeletal muscle mass, and body-fat percentage—structural change beyond scale weight.

60

常模下限——同齡群第 25 百分位，正常範圍的起點；低於此線即落後同齡水平。 The lower normative bound—the 25th percentile of your age band; below it, you trail the normal range.

85

機能儲備——同齡群第 75 百分位；以上代表超越日常負荷的安全邊際，常模並非終點。 Functional reserve—the 75th percentile of your age band; above it lies capacity beyond daily demand. The norm is not the finish line.

LOWEST

分數旁永遠並列最低分領域——限制因子所在，正是介入的起點。 The lowest domain is always named beside your score—where the limiting factor sits is where the work begins.

MILESTONES

指數於初步基線評估及其後每個既定複測節點發佈；現行訓練制度以每 16 堂為一個複測週期。複測會盡量匹配首次條件，記錄任何偏差並標註可比性；原始量值與實際變化（Δ）會一併呈現。 The index is published at your initial baseline assessment and at each subsequent defined review milestone. The current training framework uses 16-session review cycles. We match the initial conditions as closely as practical, record any deviations, and rate comparability; raw measured values and actual change (Δ) are reported together.

GOVERNANCE

透明標準，真人把關。 Transparent standards. Human accountability.

反黑盒原則 No Black Boxes

所有常模錨點均附公開文獻來源；實測數據與推算數值嚴格分級標示。 Every normative benchmark cites its published source. Measured values are strictly separated from estimates.

雙軌把關 Coach Oversight

系統協助整理數據，但所有方案判斷、安全紅線與報告發佈，均由專屬教練親自審核簽署。 Technology structures the data; our coaches own every coaching decision, safety boundary, and report release.

清晰專業邊界 Clear Scope of Practice

V2 的角色是提供客觀量度及結構化運動訓練，並非取代醫療診斷或物理治療。如涉及醫療需要、術後安排或醫學紅旗，我們會與主診醫生及註冊物理治療師協作，按其專業意見設定訓練界線；需要醫生書面許可的情況，會於取得許可後方展開訓練。 V2 provides objective measurement and structured exercise conditioning; our role does not replace medical diagnosis or physiotherapy. Where medical needs, post-operative considerations, or red flags apply, we coordinate with your physician and registered physiotherapist to establish appropriate training boundaries based on their professional guidance. Where physician clearance is required, training begins only after it has been obtained.

FAQ

預約前常見問題。 Before your first visit.

01 首次到訪會進行什麼？ What happens on the first visit? +

首次到訪為初步基線評估。我們會在可重現的標準條件下，採集你的心肺耐力、肌力輸出、平衡穩定與身體組成四個領域的客觀基線，並由專屬教練解讀個人「健康資本指數（HCI）」及鎖定首要限制因子，作為後續方案的依據。 Your first visit is an Initial Baseline Assessment. Under repeatable conditions, we measure objective baselines across cardiorespiratory capacity, strength, balance, and body composition. Your coach then reviews your Health Capital Index (HCI) and isolates the primary limiting factor to guide your next phase.

02 我沒有訓練經驗，可以開始嗎？ I have no training experience. Can I still start? +

可以。你毋須具備運動經驗，亦毋須自行判斷適合哪個方向。首次基線評估會按你的現況、風險篩查及可投入時間制定起點，其後方案亦會由教練按實際反應調整。 Yes. You do not need prior training experience, nor do you need to decide which pathway fits you. Your Initial Baseline Assessment establishes a starting point around your current condition, risk screening, and available time; your coach then adjusts the program according to your actual response.

03 有傷患、術後或長期身體狀況，可以訓練嗎？ Can I train with an injury, after surgery, or with a long-term condition? +

可以，這類情況並不少見。部分客人有術後訓練需要、長期代謝問題，或因肌力不足而在開始運動時出現關節不適。教練會先從訓練量、動作控制及肌力基線，評估可否透過訓練調整處理；如已有醫生轉介，則會與註冊物理治療師協作設定訓練界線。只有涉及診斷、治療或醫療紅旗時，才需要先轉介醫生或物理治療師。 Yes—this is common among our clients. Some join us for post-operative training, long-term metabolic concerns, or joint discomfort linked to insufficient strength when starting exercise. Your coach first assesses training volume, movement control, and strength baseline to determine whether the issue can be managed through program adjustment. Where you already have a physician's referral, we coordinate with a registered physiotherapist to establish the appropriate training boundaries. Only where diagnosis, treatment, or medical red flags are involved do we refer you to a physician or physiotherapist first.

04 收費與方案如何計算？ How are programs and fees structured? +

我們不設劃一散堂價目表。所有方案均於完成初步基線評估後，按你的實測結果、目標及行程度身報價，堂數按訓練需要編排；初步基線評估之費用可於查詢時直接向團隊確認。 We do not publish a generic drop-in price list. Programs are quoted after your Initial Baseline Assessment, tailored to your measured baselines, goals, and schedule; program length follows your training needs. Assessment fees are confirmed directly upon inquiry.

05 如何預約初步評估，或為家人轉介？ How do I book an assessment, or refer a family member? +

本中心採預約及轉介制。你可以透過下方私密查詢表單留下資料，或直接透過 WhatsApp Concierge 與我們聯絡，團隊會儘快回覆並安排時間。如你已有醫生轉介、傷患或長期身體狀況，我們亦會與你的主診醫生或註冊物理治療師協作，安排合適的訓練起點；訓練期間亦可應你的要求並在你同意下，向主診醫生提供進度摘要。 We operate strictly by appointment and referral. Submit the private inquiry form below or message our WhatsApp Concierge directly—our team responds promptly to confirm arrangements. If you already have a physician's referral, an injury, or a long-term condition, we will also coordinate with your physician or registered physiotherapist to establish an appropriate starting point for training; during training, we can also provide progress summaries to your physician upon request and with your consent.
